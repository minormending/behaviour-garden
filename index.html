Getting in here

Holding is quicker for you, and a ring fills round the button so you can see it working — the same way in as the other apps. It is the weaker of the two though: a determined child mashing buttons can stumble into a long press, where they will not guess 13 × 22.

Backup and moving

The garden lives in this browser. Nothing leaves the device unless you turn on sharing above — and then only the garden itself, never the behaviour log.

Paste a backup below. Restoring merges it in — a day that is already here is kept unless the backup grew it further, so nothing is ever lost. You can also use this to pull another parent’s garden onto this device.

Starting over

Erases every day, every flower and the behaviour log on this device. There is no undo, so take a backup first.
